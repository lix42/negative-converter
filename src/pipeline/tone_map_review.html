Metrics —

configblown %code sepmid
Lower blown% and higher code sep are better. Green beats the sigmoid, amber loses to it.

What to judge

  1. w256 vs w64. w256 scores best on blown% but is effectively classic Reinhard. If it looks flat or washed, the metrics are blind to over-compression.
  2. w64 vs sigmoid-default in the crops — better highlights, or just different?
  3. x3-hermite should look clearly broken. If it doesn't, the premise that the operator is what matters is wrong.
  4. Hard clip vs soft roll-off — do the Reinhard highlights gradate where the sigmoid's snap?
  5. Anything the metrics miss: highlight colour shifts, midtone flatness, local contrast.
